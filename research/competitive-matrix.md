# Competitive Matrix — Streaks, Habits & Micro-learning

*Compiled September 2026. Pricing and feature details change frequently — verify before using in external materials.*

## Competitors Reviewed

Duolingo, Babbel, Elevate, Memrise, and Headspace. Excluded social platforms and long-form course platforms per scope. Habit-tracker-only apps (e.g., Streaks, Habitica) were considered but excluded — they lack the micro-learning content component core to Streakly.

---

### Duolingo

- **Core features:** Gamified daily lessons across 40+ languages, plus newer Chess/Math/Music tracks. AI integration (GPT-4o-based) powers Duolingo Max, including video calls with an AI character. Advanced Stories and DuoRadio for reading/listening practice.
- **Pricing:** Free tier (ads, limited hearts, no offline). Super: ~$7.99/mo or $83.99/yr. Family plan: $119.99/yr for up to 6 users.
- **Target customer:** Broad consumer language learners, skews younger/mobile-first; free tier is the primary funnel.
- **Post-week-1 engagement:** Streak counter is central. Streak Freeze is purchasable with in-app currency (Gems); Super subscribers get "streak repair." Hearts/lives system and leaderboards add pressure and competition.
- **Notable recent changes:** Deeper AI integration (Max tier, video calls with "Lily"), expanded course depth (up to CEFR B2 in major languages), stated strategic push toward 100M daily active users by 2028.

### Babbel

- **Core features:** Structured language courses built around real-life conversation scenarios. Babbel Speak (new, AI-powered voice practice) launched in beta in late 2025, now covering 200+ scenarios across 8 categories for English, Spanish, French, Italian, German.
- **Pricing:** Monthly ~$15, 6-month ~$13/mo, 12-month ~$8-9/mo (~$107/yr). Lifetime: $299.99. Group plan: ~$186/yr for up to 6 users.
- **Target customer:** Adults learning a language for practical/travel use, generally less gamification-driven than Duolingo's audience.
- **Post-week-1 engagement:** Tracks streaks, but the research did not surface a dedicated streak-freeze or recovery mechanic — engagement leans more on structured course progression and the new Speak feature than on streak pressure.
- **Notable recent changes:** Babbel Speak (AI conversation coaching) is the major 2025–2026 addition, still in mobile-only beta as of mid-2026.

### Elevate

- **Core features:** 40+ short "brain training" games covering writing, math, and communication skills, plus a study area with tips on public speaking, speed reading, vocabulary. Weekly performance reports.
- **Pricing:** ~$9.99/mo or $39.99/yr. Free tier limited to 3 games/day.
- **Target customer:** Adults focused on cognitive/professional skill-sharpening rather than a specific subject like language.
- **Post-week-1 engagement:** Daily-streak mechanics similar to Duolingo's, plus 150+ achievements and a workout-style calendar. No dedicated streak-freeze or comeback flow surfaced in research.
- **Notable recent changes:** No major 2026 relaunch found; incremental content and reporting updates.

### Memrise

- **Core features:** Vocabulary-focused language learning via flashcards, spaced repetition, and native-speaker video clips. "Mems" feature (AI-generated visual memory aids) relaunched Feb 2026. AI Buddies (conversation practice, Pro-only) added 2026.
- **Pricing:** Pro around $24.99/mo or $61.99/yr as of mid-2026 (note: some sources show lower legacy pricing, ~$8.49/mo — pricing appears to be in flux post-redesign). Lifetime: $329.99.
- **Target customer:** Vocabulary-focused language learners who want a lighter, less gamified experience than Duolingo.
- **Post-week-1 engagement:** Streak-based accountability with a "plant-growing" metaphor and reminders; a "Difficult Words" review resurfaces struggling terms automatically. No explicit streak-freeze found. Community-created courses were recently retired, narrowing content to official courses only — a controversial change per user commentary.
- **Notable recent changes:** Full app redesign (cloud-based, no offline mode), retirement of community courses, new AI Buddies feature.

### Headspace

- **Core features:** 1,000+ guided meditations, themed multi-week programs, standalone "Singles" for specific moments, monthly check-ins using validated stress/anxiety scales.
- **Pricing:** ~$12.99/mo or $69.99/yr.
- **Target customer:** Broad wellness/mindfulness audience, often recommended for stress and sleep, not skill acquisition specifically.
- **Post-week-1 engagement:** Tracks consecutive-day streaks and total minutes, but pairs this with explicitly low-pressure messaging — the app's own guidance tells users not to feel guilty about a missed day. Mindful reminders nudge users back at a scheduled time.
- **Notable recent changes:** Continued expansion of guided programs and check-in/progress-tracking tools; no major streak-mechanic overhaul found.

## White Space for Streakly

1. **A free, built-in streak-recovery moment, not a paid workaround.** The only competitor with an explicit protection mechanic (Duolingo's Streak Freeze) gates it behind in-app currency or a paid tier. Nobody reviewed offers a no-cost, designed-in "comeback" moment. Streakly's proposed Comeback screen (best-streak stat, short lesson, one-tap freeze) would be differentiated by being part of the core product, not a monetized add-on.

2. **A re-entry experience that acknowledges where the user actually is.** Every competitor reviewed treats "still on a streak" and "returning after a lapse" as the same home-screen state — the tone shift Headspace offers is in copy/messaging, not in a tailored flow. None personalize the return experience based on how long a user was away. This is the same gap Streakly's own NPS feedback surfaced independently ("home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away").
